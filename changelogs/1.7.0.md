## Features
- New `/groups` command to manage language, timezone, and notification settings for your groups in one place (replaces `/language`)
- Set your own language for the bot's direct messages, separate from the group language
- Opt out of game results and leaderboard tracking
- Group admin cat turn leaderboard notifications on or off per group
- Automatic final court check 15 minutes before the deadline that releases any courts no longer needed by the current player count
- More responsive and reliable bot
